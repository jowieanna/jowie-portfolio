02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Intentional by design.

I’m a Product Designer interested in the space where people, technology and business meet. I enjoy getting close to a problem, untangling complex workflows and making decisions that help a product feel clearer and more useful.

My work has also taken me beyond interface design. I've helped shape feature requirements, identify gaps, write user stories and acceptance criteria, coordinate with developers and follow work through testing and iteration. I bring a product mindset to design — with a strong appreciation for the craft of making things.
